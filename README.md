## ✨ What Does poyra Do For You?

poyra simplifies three critical financial tasks that normally require multiple tools or manual spreadsheet work:

- **Smart Payment Routing** – Automatically sends each transaction to the most cost-effective payment provider, saving you money on every sale
- **Three-Way Reconciliation** – Automatically matches your bank statements, payment provider reports, and your own records, eliminating hours of manual checking
- **Commission Monitoring** – Tracks what each payment provider charges you and alerts you to any unexpected fees
- **Value Date Tracking** – Shows you exactly when each payment will reach your bank account, helping you manage cash flow

## 🛠️ Key Features

### 🧠 Intelligent Payment Routing

poyra looks at every transaction and decides which payment provider (sanal POS) will give you the best rate and highest success rate. This happens automatically in the background, so you don't need to make complex decisions.

### 🔄 Effortless Reconciliation

At the end of each day, poyra automatically compares:
- What your payment providers say they processed
- What your bank says arrived
- What your records show

Any mismatches are highlighted clearly, and poyra suggests fixes.

### 💰 Commission & Fee Control

You define the commission rates for each provider once. From then on, poyra calculates fees automatically, shows you monthly totals, and flags any charges that don't match the agreed rates.

### 📅 Clear Valör Information

Every payment in poyra shows when the money is actually available in your account. No more guessing or calling your bank.

## 🧭 How to Use poyra (Basic Workflow)

### Step 1: Connect Your Payment Providers

After installation, poyra guides you through connecting your existing payment providers (sanal POS systems). You'll enter your provider credentials once, and poyra handles everything else.

### Step 2: Import or Connect Your Bank Account

Link your business bank account to automatically receive daily statements. If you prefer manual imports, you can upload bank statement files directly.

### Step 3: Let poyra Work Its Magic

Once connected, poyra runs automatically:
- Routes new transactions intelligently
- Post-processes each day's payments
- Creates clear reports you can review anytime

### Step 4: Review Daily Reports

Each morning, you'll see a summary of:
- Yesterday's transactions
- Any discrepancies found
- Fees charged by providers
- Expected arrival dates for your funds

## 🧰 Troubleshooting Common Issues

**Issue:** The application doesn't open after installation.
**Solution:** Make sure you have the latest version installed. Check Windows Updates for any pending updates.

**Issue:** My payment provider isn't connecting.
**Solution:** Verify your provider credentials are correct. Some providers require additional setup in their portal first.

**Issue:** Reports show wrong amounts.
**Solution:** Click "Re-sync" on the reconciliation page. poyra will re-fetch all data and recalculate.
